### MVP data controller authorization contract

`ResourceScopeGuard` lets admin/coordinator manage tenant flow definitions, versions, availability and intent mappings after replacing caller-supplied company scope with the current DB actor's company. Other tenant roles are denied; superadmin retains cross-company access.
